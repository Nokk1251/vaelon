# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge
Everyone who takes part in this project - in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space - should
be able to do so without harassment, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of
experience, nationality, appearance, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and patient; assume good intent.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.
- Keep discussions on topic.

## Not acceptable
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, sexualised language or unwelcome advances.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Asking for help with attacking systems you do not own, malware, or cloning a real person's voice.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break these rules, and may
block people who repeat such behaviour. Report a problem privately to the maintainer (see the repository's contact
details). Reports are handled confidentially.
